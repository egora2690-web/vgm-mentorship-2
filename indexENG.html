How to find a mentor

Step 1

Choose a mentor

Find a suitable specialist in our catalog of verified mentors. You can filter them by field, experience, and other criteria.

Step 2

Write to them

Leave a request on the website. Try to clearly formulate your request — this will increase the chances that the mentor will agree to work with you.

Step 3

It's up to you

After the mentor approves your request, you can arrange a meeting. The system will create a chat link for convenient communication.

How to become
a mentor

Step 1

Click the "Become a mentor" button. Register by selecting your field of activity and describing your experience.

Step 2

Your profile will be checked by university moderators for compliance with the competencies of the direction.

Step 3

After verification, you will appear in the catalog. You will be able to accept requests and schedule meetings with students.
