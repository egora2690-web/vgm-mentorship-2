How to find a mentor

Step 1

Choose a mentor

Find a suitable specialist in our catalog of verified mentors. You can filter them by direction, experience and other parameters.

Step 2

Write to them

Submit a request on the site. Try to state your request clearly — it increases the chances that the mentor agrees to work with you.

Step 3

It's up to you

Once the mentor approves your request, you can arrange a meeting. The system will create a chat link for convenient communication.

How to become a mentor

Step 1

Click “Become a mentor”. Register, choosing your field of activity and describing your experience.

Step 2

Your profile will be reviewed by university moderators for compliance with the competencies of the field.

Step 3

After review you will appear in the catalog. You will be able to accept requests and schedule meetings with students.
