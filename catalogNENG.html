Research Program

Research Mentorship

The program is designed to integrate VolgSMU students into the academic environment and support them in implementing their first serious research projects.

"Experienced mentors and university scientists will help you navigate the path from the first hypothesis to publication in prestigious scientific journals indexed in VAK and Scopus."

Key areas of support:

  • Assistance in choosing a relevant research topic and proper experimental planning.
  • Training in searching, correctly analyzing, and systematizing international scientific publications.
  • Support in preparing abstracts, articles, and presentations for defense at conferences.
