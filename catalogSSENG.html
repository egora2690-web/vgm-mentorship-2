Adaptation Program

Student-Student Direction

This program is designed specifically for first-year and junior students of VolgSMU who find it difficult to adapt to the demanding pace and overwhelming amount of information at a medical university.

"Senior students act as tutors, helping to avoid retakes and panic before exams."

How mentors can help:

  • Breaking down complex topics in anatomy, histology, biochemistry, and Latin.
  • Proven tips and tricks for preparing for colloquia and practical exams.
  • Assistance with effective memorization of medical terminology.
