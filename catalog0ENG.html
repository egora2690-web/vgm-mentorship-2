Practical Program

Resident-Student Direction

The transition from dry university theory to real clinical practice is the most difficult stage for senior students. Clinical residents will help you feel confident at the patient's bedside.

"Learn real medicine from those who were recently students themselves but already work in the clinical departments of the city's hospitals."

Areas of work:

  • Mastering practical medical procedures and basic patient examination algorithms.
  • Learning proper management, completion, and analysis of medical records and case histories.
  • Analysis of real complex clinical cases directly in medical departments.
